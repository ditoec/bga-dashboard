# Bumitama / BGA Group — Brand Reference

**What this is:** a portable, working brand reference distilled from the visual
language of Bumitama Agri Ltd.'s Annual Report 2025 — color values measured
directly from its printed pages, typographic and tonal patterns observed
across its layouts. **It is not an official brand book** (Bumitama Agri Ltd.
has not published one to us), so treat every value here as a well-sourced
approximation, not a certified spec. If pixel-perfect brand compliance
matters (investor decks, press materials), get the source logo files and
official Pantone/CMYK values from the company directly.

Use this file as a drop-in style reference for other tools: paste the
relevant section into a slide-deck theme, a Word/report template, an
image-generation prompt, or a new web project.

## 2. Logo (described, not reproduced)

The mark is an abstract stylised leaf/feather form, rendered as a gradient
sweep from green at the base to gold/amber at the tip, with a curved
negative-space "ribbon" motif running through it. It sits above a serif
wordmark ("Bumitama Agri Ltd.") in the primary green, with the tagline
"Excellence Through Discipline" set in a small gray sans-serif beneath.

**Do not attempt to recreate this exact mark** — it's a registered
trademark. For your own materials, use a simple text lockup (wordmark +
tagline, per §4) or a generic monogram instead, in the brand colors below.

## 3. Color palette

Colors below were sampled directly (pixel color-picked) from the Annual
Report 2025's vector graphics, so they're accurate to that document, not
independently verified against a Pantone book.

### Primary

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Signature Green** | `#0A7D40` | Wordmark, primary brand color. Use for headlines, primary buttons, key accents. |
| Green (mid tint) | `#218952` | Secondary green step |
| Green (light tint) | `#60AA83` | Tints/backgrounds, subtle fills |
| **Deep Forest Green** | `#1E2F24` (measured range `#213126`–`#263429`) | Dark backgrounds for feature/hero spreads — this is the "dark mode" of the brand, not a tint of the signature green |

### Accent (gold / amber family)

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Amber Gold** | `#A9831D` | Primary data/chart accent (bars, secondary headings on dark backgrounds) |
| Deep Gold | `#CA981A` | Secondary chart line, hover/active states |
| Bright Gold-Yellow | `#FFF420` | **High-emphasis accent only** — used sparingly for a single highlighted data line or callout, never as a fill or body color. Treat as a "hero" accent, not part of the everyday palette. |
| Burnt Orange | `#E5732E` | Icon/illustration accent, secondary to gold |

### Neutrals

| Role | Hex | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Standard page background (content pages) |
| Gray (secondary text) | `#828285` | Taglines, captions, secondary copy on white |
| Gray (light) | `#AEAEB0` | Tertiary text, hairlines |
| Gray (pale) | `#C1C1C3` | Dividers, disabled states |

### How the two "modes" actually work in the source material

The report itself alternates between two looks, not a simple light/dark
toggle of one palette:

- **Content pages:** white background, signature green for headings, gray
  body text, amber/gold used only in charts and small accents.
- **Feature/financial spreads:** deep forest green background, gold/amber
  for headline numbers and chart bars, white/cream for body text, bright
  gold-yellow reserved for the single most important data line.

Port both modes together (as a light theme and a dark theme) rather than
picking one — that alternation is a real, recognizable part of the system.

## 4. Typography

Exact typeface names aren't identifiable from a rendered PDF, so these are
**descriptive matches** with portable substitutes that are free to use
anywhere (Google Fonts, Office, image-gen prompts).

| Role | Character | Free substitute |
|---|---|---|
| **Display / wordmark** | Bold serif, moderate contrast, slightly condensed — reads as trustworthy/editorial rather than decorative | **Fraunces** (600–700 weight), or Source Serif 4, PT Serif |
| **Headings** | Same serif family as display, lighter weight | Fraunces 500–600 |
| **Body / UI** | Clean, humanist sans-serif, no display quirks | **Inter**, or system-ui / Segoe UI / -apple-system |
| **Tagline / captions** | Same sans, small size, gray, wide-ish letter spacing | Inter, small caps or letter-spacing +0.04em |

Pairing rule: **serif for anything that names the brand or leads a
section (headlines, big numbers); sans for everything functional** (body
copy, labels, data). Never use the serif for dense body paragraphs.

## 5. Tone of voice

- **Measured, not hyped.** Claims are backed by a specific number and a
  time frame ("net gearing down for the sixth consecutive year," not
  "record-breaking growth").
- **Discipline is the recurring virtue word.** Operational discipline,
  capital discipline, agronomic discipline — the motto isn't decorative,
  it's load-bearing across the whole narrative.
- **Long horizon.** Prefers multi-year framing (CAGR, five-year highlights,
  "through cycles") over single-period wins.
- **Sustainability as infrastructure, not a campaign.** Framed alongside
  production metrics as an operating discipline, with specific figures
  (hectares certified, tonnes of CO2e), not aspirational language.

Sample sentence patterns to imitate:
> "[Metric] reached [number], a [%] increase from [prior period], reflecting
> [operational driver] rather than one-off factors."

> "The Group remains attentive to [long-term consideration], positioning it
> to [outcome] through [external condition]."

## 6. Imagery & iconography

- **Photography (if you have licensed access to real photography):** aerial
  estate/mill shots, warm natural light, human scale kept small relative to
  the landscape. Not used here — see disclaimer at top.
- **Illustration style on financial pages:** engraved/etched-line icons
  (coins, upward arrows, gears, handshake) in gold-on-dark, decorative but
  literal (a coin means money, an arrow means growth) — avoid abstract or
  overly modern iconography if matching this style.
- **Decorative pattern:** a fine batik-inspired linework pattern, gold on
  dark green, used as a low-contrast background texture on feature spreads
  — never as a foreground element.
- **Data charts:** bar + line combination charts (bar = absolute value,
  line = a second related metric or trend), gold bars, bright accent line,
  CAGR called out as a headline label above the chart, values labeled
  directly on bars/points rather than relying on a Y-axis alone.

## 7. Applying this elsewhere

**PowerPoint / Keynote theme**
- Slide background: white (content) or Deep Forest Green `#1E2F24` (section dividers/closing)
- Title font: Fraunces 600, Signature Green `#0A7D40` on white / white on green
- Body font: Inter, Gray `#828285` on white / light gray on green
- Accent for charts/callouts: Amber Gold `#A9831D`; use Bright Gold-Yellow only for one hero metric per deck

**Word/report template**
- Headings in Fraunces, Signature Green
- Body in Inter or Calibri/Segoe UI (closest system default)
- Pull-quotes or key stats in a shaded Deep Forest Green box, white/gold text
- Tables: white background, green header row, gold for positive deltas

**Image-generation prompts**
Drop this in as a style descriptor:
> "Corporate agricultural branding, deep forest green (#1E2F24) and amber
> gold (#A9831D) color scheme, bold serif headline type, clean sans-serif
> body text, engraved gold line-art icons, subtle batik-inspired linework
> texture, warm and disciplined editorial tone, no bright saturated colors
> except sparing golden-yellow highlights."

**Web** — see this repo's own `css/style.css` (dashboard) and `css/site.css`
(public site) for a working, accessibility-validated implementation of this
exact system in both light and dark mode.

## 8. Do / Don't

**Do**
- Use the green + gold + white/dark-green system consistently across both
  "modes" described in §3.
- Reserve the bright gold-yellow for one hero data point at a time.
- Pair serif headlines with sans body text — never mix within the same role.
- Back up claims with a specific number and time frame.

**Don't**
- Don't recreate or trace the actual logo mark — it's trademarked.
- Don't use real photography or headshots from the Annual Report without
  the rights holder's explicit permission.
- Don't use the bright gold-yellow as a large fill or background — it's an
  accent, not a base color.
- Don't mix in unrelated hues (blues, purples) — the entire system is
  green/gold/neutral by design.
