About the Group

Three decades in the field, one operating philosophy.

Since acquiring its first concession in Central Kalimantan in 1996, BGA Group has grown into a vertically-focused upstream palm oil producer — cultivating, harvesting and milling exclusively, without moving into refining or consumer products. That focus has shaped a culture built entirely around agronomic discipline and capital stewardship.

The Group's estates sit within agronomically favoured landscapes with balanced rainfall and a mature age profile, positioning them well within their peak productive years. Nucleus estates work alongside plasma smallholder partnerships that exceed government-mandated thresholds, weaving local communities into a long-term, shared value chain.

"Excellence Through Discipline."
